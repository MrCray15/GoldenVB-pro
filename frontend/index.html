About Us

A private escape in the heart of Malolos.

Golden Villa is a private resort in Malolos, Bulacan, built for families, barkadas, and small celebrations that deserve a place of their own. One group per day, no strangers sharing your pool, no loud crowd you didn't invite.

The villa is surrounded by gardens and open sky, with a full-size pool, shaded lounging areas, and enough room for up to thirty guests. Whether it's a birthday, a reunion, or just a weekend away from Manila, the space is yours for the day — or the night.

We opened Golden Villa to share a place we've always loved. Every booking is handled personally — you'll talk to us from your first inquiry to the moment you arrive at the gate.
